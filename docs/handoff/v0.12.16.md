# v0.12.16 — a mix no longer starts in the middle of a rise of the outgoing song when the incoming song starts quiet (21/10)

**The owner (21/10, two screenshots):** sometimes the outgoing song starts to rise in bass and drums, and exactly there the mix begins with an incoming song that is quiet and has only singing, the music coming in later. The energy drops in the middle of the rise.

**Cause:** a plain mix joins on A's next phrase start (`ctl.deckPhraseJoin`), and a build-up often sits right behind a phrase start. B's entry is picked on B's own contour. Neither side looked at the other.

**What was built:** `core/rise-guard.ts`. From the two energy contours: A "rises" if its top within 16 s after the join is at least 1.25 times its level in the 6 s before, and at least 0.08 of its near-peak higher; B is "quiet" if its first stretch of the mix is under 0.6 of that top. Both together cut a rise. The join then moves to A's next phrase start, up to 6 tries; if all cut, the first is kept and the notice says so. The panel shows the same fact under each entry point. It is a loudness reading, and the text says so.

**Experiment before building (pass rule set first):** all ordered pairs of the 6 staged real songs, every 8-bar phrase start of A against every energy point of B, 404 combinations. Rule: fires on 3-40% (not never, not always) and moves to a clean join at least 60% of the time it fires. Result: fired 25.7% (104 of 404), 78% of those found a clean join (81 of 104). Passed. This is a plausibility check on 6 songs (404 combinations, not independent; thresholds were set on these same songs; "rescued" only means the same rule stopped firing at a later phrase start), not a validation — only the owner's ear says whether a firing was a real cut. The experiment script lives in the session scratchpad, not in the repo. Not tested against the owner's own pair (Nightshapes to Higher Calling): its analysis lives on his PC.

**Known limits:** B's quiet window is read at the chosen entry second, not at the second the bar shift or kick lock may move it to. When a song has no energy reading yet, or the join is on A's last phrase, the check does not run and the notice and panel say so (`unchecked`).

**Not changed:** the vocal handoff (its own order, v0.12.13) and which entry point B uses. Only when A is joined.

**Check:** `tests/core/rise-guard.test.ts`, `npm run check` green, `scripts/verify-vocal-handoff.mjs` 45/45 in the running app.

## Good example from the owner (21/10)

"I Don_t Wanna Wait" (David Guetta and OneRepublic, A) into "My Mind - Korolova JOA" (B), a vocal handoff he called a perfect mix. Both songs read from his Tracks folder. The rule does not fire at any join from 20 s to 48 s of A with B entering at 0 s or 21 s: A's rise ratio at most 1.13 (needs 1.25), B's entry share at least 0.67 (quiet is under 0.6). A's own lift sits at about 15 s, before every one of those joins. So this mix is a true negative for the rule; it is one pair, not a calibration.
